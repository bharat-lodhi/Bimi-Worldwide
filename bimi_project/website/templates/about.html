OUR GLOBAL SOURCING PROCESS

From Requirement to Global Delivery

At BIMI Worldwide, we manage every stage of international sourcing—from understanding your requirements to delivering quality products at your destination.

01

Understanding Your Requirement

We begin by understanding your exact product specifications, quality standards, quantity requirements, destination, and delivery timeline to ensure the right sourcing strategy.

02

Global Supplier Sourcing

Using our international supplier network, we identify reliable manufacturers, exporters, and producers capable of meeting your business requirements.

03

Supplier Verification

Every supplier is evaluated based on manufacturing capability, certifications, production capacity, export experience, and business credibility before selection.

04

Quality Inspection

Products undergo quality inspections during production and before shipment to ensure they comply with your specifications and international standards.

05

Packaging & Labeling

We coordinate export-standard packaging, customized labeling, palletization, and container loading to protect products throughout transit.

06

Documentation & Compliance

Our team manages export documentation including Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Fumigation Certificate, Inspection Certificates, Bill of Lading, and other country-specific compliance requirements.

07

International Logistics

We arrange sea freight, air freight, customs clearance, inland transportation, shipment tracking, and logistics coordination through our global network.

08

Delivery & After-Sales Support

Your shipment reaches its destination safely and on time. Our team continues to provide shipment updates, documentation support, and post-delivery assistance whenever required.
