How It Works

Step-by-step onboarding process to join the BIMI Worldwide supplier network.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete the supplier registration form with your company information, product categories, certifications, and manufacturing capabilities.

02

Profile Review

Our sourcing team reviews your business profile, production capacity, export experience, and product portfolio.

03

Verification Process

We may conduct a virtual meeting or factory verification to understand your quality systems, manufacturing standards, and operational capabilities.

04

Documentation Review

Our compliance team verifies the following required documents:

  • Company Registration
  • GST / VAT Registration
  • IEC (If Applicable)
  • Product Certifications
  • Factory Profile
  • Quality Certificates
05

Supplier Approval

Once approved, your company becomes part of the BIMI Worldwide Supplier Network and may receive sourcing inquiries from our international buyers.

06

Start Growing Together

Receive business inquiries, collaborate with our sourcing specialists, and build long-term export partnerships with clients worldwide.
